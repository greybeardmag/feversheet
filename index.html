Who We Are

A Team Dedicated to Children's Health

CarePoint Health is a community-based pediatric clinic staffed by board-certified physicians with subspecialty training in Pediatric Emergency Medicine. We believe every child deserves timely, evidence-based care delivered with warmth and respect — regardless of language, background, or ability to pay.

  • Evidence-based treatment protocols
  • Interpreter services in 14 languages
  • Family-centred approach to care
